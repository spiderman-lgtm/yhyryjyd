What it is

Your team's standups, requests and reviews — in one calm place.

SquadForge reads your team's Slack channel, understands what everyone is working on, and turns it into a live dashboard, a 7 PM daily alert and a Monday report. Eight small agents do the reading and writing, so you can spend your time leading instead of chasing updates.

How it works

Slack in, clarity out.

  1. 1 · SlackMorning and evening standup threads, client requests you post and tag people on, and status words like r&d, started, stuck, completed.
  2. 2 · AgentsScout syncs the channel, Tracker follows each request, Inspector reviews work with your own instructions, Analyst finds what's blocked.
  3. 3 · DashboardWhat shipped, what's moving, who's blocked and why — live, with a Village and Live Flow view of every request's journey.
  4. 4 · ReportsA 7 PM alert of who did what (and who hasn't posted), a Monday team report and a personal weekly report for everyone.

Fairness

Built to help people, not watch them.

🚫 Never a rankingNo leaderboards and no “top performer”. Everyone is seen against their own work.
🧭 Stuck is contextA blocker comes with its reason and who can unblock it — never a mark against someone.
💬 Every flag has a reasonWhen Inspector asks for a fix, it says exactly what and why, using the rules you wrote.
👀 Nothing hiddenOnly the channel you choose is read — no screenshots, keystrokes or time tracking.
🧮 Volume doesn't countTen small tasks don't beat one shipped request. Outcomes and evidence do.
✋ You stay in chargeAgents draft and suggest; nothing goes out to the team until you send it.
Our principle

Value, not volume.

More tasks don't mean more productivity. SquadForge measures what work achieved — what shipped, what got unblocked, the outcome for the client — never a ranking, never a penalty. Stuck is context, never a fault.

Where you work

The tools your team already uses.

No new app for your team to learn. They keep working in Slack; SquadForge listens, and sends alerts where people will see them.
