How it works

One day with SquadForge.

Your team keeps working in Slack. SquadForge runs the routine around it.

  1. ☀️ 11:00 AMA good-morning message opens the day's thread. Everyone gets an M91 alert with yesterday's numbers and posts today's plan.
  2. 🛠️ During the dayRequests move from the thread — R&D, started, stuck (with the reason), completed. Website plug requests are picked up too.
  3. 🔍 When an app is postedInspector reviews it against your rules and replies in the thread: what's right, the mistakes, what's missing.
  4. 📢 7:00 PMYou get the day's summary. Anyone without an update gets a gentle M91 nudge.
  5. 📜 MondayA full record of the week — work built, targets met, reviews and common mistakes — for you and your boss.
