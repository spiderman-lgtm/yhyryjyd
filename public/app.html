📱 Your WhatsApp Business number
Via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Connect it once — the AI will answer your customers.
—

How to connect your number (5 min)
  1. developers.facebook.com → Create app → type Business → add WhatsApp.
  2. WhatsApp → API Setup: add and verify your business number, then copy its Phone number ID.
  3. Access token: Business Settings → System users → generate a token (permission: whatsapp_business_messaging, whatsapp_business_management). The temporary token expires in 24 hours — use a permanent one.
  4. App settings → Basic → copy the App secret (used to verify the webhook).
  5. WhatsApp → Configuration → Webhook: copy the Callback URL and Verify token below, then subscribe to the messages field.
