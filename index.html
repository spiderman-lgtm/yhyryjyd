School & Coaching Institute SaaS · powered by viaSocket

ShikshaFlow

Staff use the app as usual. Fee reminders, absent alerts, admission sync and result messages go out to parents automatically through viaSocket flows.

How it works

The app owns the data. viaSocket owns the integrations. A new integration is a new flow, not new backend code.

1 · Action in the appTeacher marks attendance, admin publishes a result, parent submits a form, scheduler finds a due fee
2 · Eventfee.due, attendance.absent, admission.submitted, result.published
3 · viaSocket flowWebhook trigger → filter → actions, with retries and an idempotency key
4 · Parent / teamWhatsApp, SMS, email, payment link, Google Sheet, CRM lead
