Add any app · viaSocket rubric

Owner koi bhi app jodna chahe — pehle AI check karega ki use case genuine hai ya nahi.

viaSocket ke plugin-validator rubric pe chalta hai: verdict, score /100, connector decision, 3 Trigger → Action → Result use cases, risk. Sirf GO pe app judta hai — dukaan mein kachra nahi.
